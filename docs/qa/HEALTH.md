# Health

Last reviewed: 2026-08-03
Role: active working

## When to use this checklist

Use when a ticket changes the Health page, health summaries, custom foods, recipes, meals, water, imports, or Health persistence.

## Checklist

### General Health

- [ ] The Health page loads without obvious missing cards, blank panels, or crashes.
- [ ] Key health summaries and widgets display plausible values.
- [ ] Health actions or imports, when touched by the change, respond as expected.

### Foods, recipes, and meals

- [ ] A custom food can be created, edited, logged today, and removed without changing an existing meal-history snapshot.
- [ ] A recipe calculates nutrition per serving from ingredient quantities and batch yield.
- [ ] A custom meal can combine foods and recipe servings and log to its default meal slot.

### Water and persistence

- [ ] Water quick-add and custom amounts accept cups and US fluid ounces, show equivalent same-day totals in both units, and allow an individual entry to be removed.
- [ ] Refreshing preserves custom foods, recipes, meals, and water in the active local or remote Health storage mode.
